# Course data sources

Hole numbers below are lengths in feet and pars read from public course pages (own wording; no maps or images copied).
Geometry in the JSON (tee/basket positions, bends, trees, ponds, terrain) is still an approximation for every course
except Picnic Island and Olympus, whose geometry now comes from UDisc's own pin data (see the Picnic Island and Olympus
sections below): each other hole keeps its old direction and was rescaled so tee-to-basket distance matches the real length.

## Real lengths and pars

* **Picnic Island** (Tampa, FL): UDisc layout "Orange Pads/Orange Baskets", par 57, 4,075 ft.
  https://udisc.com/courses/picnic-island-30nO . Disc Golf Course Review lists par 60 over A/B/C pins (two par 4s on A,
  seven on C); per-hole numbers for other pins not found. Notes: tide defines OB on two bayside fairways, 2 water hazards,
  wind matters, mostly flat.
* **Olympus** (Brooksville, FL): UDisc layout "Red to Red", par 61, 5,920 ft (13 layouts exist, incl. 24-hole long course).
  https://udisc.com/courses/olympus-dgc-u7x5 . Hosts DGPT Supreme Flight Open (2026 schedule:
  https://discgolffanatic.com/2026-disc-golf-pro-tour-schedule/). No per-hole OB/water notes found.
* **Cypress Point Park** (Tampa, FL): UDisc layout "Short Pads", par 64, 6,525 ft.
  https://udisc.com/courses/cypress-point-park-eSXz . PDGA directory: 18 holes, 7,980 ft longest, Climo/King 2014.
  https://www.pdga.com/course-directory/course/cypress-point-park-disc-golf-course . The "Cypress Long" layout
  (par 64, 7,393 ft) was used for MPO in the 2023 Gulf Coast Tour Challenge; per-hole numbers for it not found.

## Picnic Island geometry (satellite-traced, rebuilt)

Everything below is regenerated by `python3 tools/picnic_build.py` (inputs: `tools/picnic_udisc_raw.txt`, `tools/picnic_spec.py`);
check with `python3 tools/validate_picnic.py`; per-hole top-down PNGs with `python3 tools/picnic_preview.py` (`tools/picnic_preview/`,
drawn in the same rotated frame as the UDisc screenshots so they can be flipped against them).

**Pins and bends: exact, not traced.** UDisc's course-map page carries the latitude/longitude of every tee, basket and dogleg
point of all four layouts (Short Orange / Long Blue pads, A / B / Blue baskets), plus par and length per tee-basket pair. These were
read from the page data and converted to metres (x east, z north, origin 27.8548 N, 82.5520 W, equirectangular). The JSON
tees/baskets match them to under 0.15 m.
Layouts: UDisc's "Orange Pads/Orange Baskets" (Short Orange to A) sums to exactly par 57 and 4,075 ft with B as the stand-in on
holes 3, 7-11 and 15 (no A path there), which is how that layout is built; the other three pairings give par 57 / 5,495 ft,
par 61 / 5,594 ft and par 61 / 7,024 ft. Doglegs that differ per layout became extra hole entries (number + 100 or + 200,
"alt line") when one shared `via` list could not follow all paths within 8 m: holes 2, 6, 10, 11, 13.

**Trees, bushes, water: hand-read from imagery (Maxar via Mapbox, UDisc satellite view).** For each hole the Mapbox map was set
to a rotated view (tee left, basket right) at zoom 19.2-19.7 (about 0.12-0.18 m per screenshot pixel), a screenshot taken, and
canopy masses, lone canopies, scrub and the waterline outlined in screenshot pixels. Pixels become metres through the exact
Mapbox view (centre, zoom, bearing) used for that screenshot, not by eye. Check on 15 tee/basket icons that were read in the
screenshots: mean 0.6 m, rms 0.8 m, worst 2.0 m. Reading the canopy edges is rougher: allow about 2 m for outlines of masses, and
lone canopies are about 2-3 m. Inside an outlined mass the individual trees are NOT surveyed: they are seeded Poisson-disc fills
(spacing 4.5-6 m, canopy 2.4-4.5 m, height from canopy), so the cluster is right but single trunks are not. Only trees within
32 m of a line are kept, nearest 90 per hole (cap, for phone performance); a hole with 90 trees is cropped by that cap, not by
the imagery. `clear` keeps 2-3.5 m of lane around the line in the woods holes. Bushes (scrub patches) appear on holes 1 and 11
only. Shoreline: the bay is chains of 30 m circular ponds along the waterline read on holes 1, 11, 17 and 18, and the terrain grid
is rebuilt (land 1.5 m, bay -0.3 m) from a west waterline read off a zoom 16.9 overview, so allow roughly 10 m there. Tide
means the real waterline moves; the hole 1/17/18 sand strips are treated as dry. The Blvd road beside holes 8-10 and the car
parks are not modelled (no road/OB key in the format).

Per-hole notes (L/R = turn direction of the dogleg as played, with the turn angle; confidence is for tree placement):

* 1 (45-117 m, par 3): straight, along the beach edge; mangrove/oak wall on the inland side, small dark clumps between the line and
  the sand; scrub patch east of B. Bay 15-20 m beside the line. Layout high, trees medium.
* 2 (104-147 m, par 4): open picnic lawn, woods on both ends; Long Blue to A bends R19 and Short Orange to Blue bends R28 (two alt
  lines); oaks beside the car park south of the line. Layout high, trees medium.
* 3 (43-69 m, par 3): short shot across a small clearing in dense live-oak woods, straight. Medium.
* 4 (53-88 m, par 3): straight across the lawn next to the main car park; woods begin right of the baskets, oak clump left of the tee.
  Medium.
* 5 (73-130 m): straight to A/B; Blue is a par 4 with a slight bend (L1 short, R13 long). Lawn with scattered oaks, woods behind tees.
  Medium.
* 6 (66-168 m): A/B straight par 3; Blue is a par 4 bending R25 (short) or R12 then R17 (long, two bends, alt lines). Open park with
  tall pines; woods behind the long tee. Medium.
* 7 (48-79 m, par 3): short, dense woods all round, S-B bends L12. Medium-low (canopy hides the lane).
* 8 (80-152 m): hugs the inner edge of Picnic Island Blvd; bends L17-L19 around the roadside oaks (Blue is a par 4). Medium.
* 9 (78-96 m, par 3): straight across the lawn above the road, pine/palm clusters, dense woods behind tees. Medium.
* 10 (68-155 m): lawn with pines beside the road; Short Orange to Blue is par 4 and bends L19 (alt line); woods above. Medium.
* 11 (101-199 m, par 4): the wrap-around hole. Long tees run up beside the road, bend (L86 then L37 for Long Blue to Blue) and cut
  across the open field; scrub patches by B/Blue; bay and boat ramp on the far side. Medium-high layout, medium trees.
* 12 (51-74 m, par 3): from woods edge to a grassy/sandy glade; bends L23-L26 to A and about L66-L69 to Blue up a trail. Medium.
* 13 (61-103 m, par 3): a lane through oaks into a glade; Short Orange to A/Blue bends L26. Alt line for the long tee. Medium.
* 14 (47-87 m, par 3): tight lane through dense oaks; Long Blue to A bends R36. Medium-low.
* 15 (121-179 m, par 4): the long open-meadow hole, straight; woods at the tee side and lower-left. Medium.
* 16 (69-105 m, par 3): wide open lawn by the car park; one big oak mid-fairway and a clump around B/Blue. Medium-high.
* 17 (71-108 m, par 3): beach edge; both A paths bend R27-R30 around two big oak clumps south of the lane; a lone oak on the sand.
  High for layout and water, medium-high trees.
* 18 (63-85 m, par 3): dead straight, open bayside meadow, three palms just south of the lane, sand/shallows 13 m off the tee.
  High layout, medium trees.

What is not known: hole-by-hole real fairway width (set from the visible mown corridor, 6-18 m), real tree species/heights (set from
canopy size), whether the oaks overhang the lines at their true heights, and any ground height (flat, no survey).

## Olympus (Brooksville, FL): rebuilt from UDisc pins and the Jomez DiscGolf broadcast videos

Regenerate with `python3 tools/olympus_build.py` (inputs `tools/olympus_spec.py`; previews `python3 tools/olympus_preview.py` ->
`tools/olympus_preview/`). The old file (traced relative positions, invented bends, bushes, ponds and OB) is replaced.

**Pins and bends: exact (UDisc).** The course-map page (udisc.com/courses/olympus-dgc-u7x5/v2/course-map) carries lat/lon for every
tee (Gold, Silver, Red, ...), basket (Blue, Red, Old/New variants) and dogleg point, plus par and length per tee-basket pair; these
are copied into `RAW` in the spec and projected to metres (origin lat 28.5565, lon -82.3090).
Two layouts share the land:
* **MPO Supreme Flight Open** (holes 1-18): Gold tee to Blue basket (hole 2 uses New Blue), par 66. Par and tournament length come from
  the broadcast title cards (front nine 3,4,4,4,4,3,3,3,3; back nine seen: 10 par 3, 11 par 4, 12 par 3, 13 par 3, 15 par 5, 17 par 4;
  holes 14 par 5, 16 par 4 and 18 par 4 are UDisc's). Broadcast lengths (m): 106, 239, 165, 220, 192, 98, 110, 136, 146, 113, 166, 162,
  138, -, 293, -, 168, -. Where the broadcast distance differs from UDisc's Gold-Blue path by more than 3 % (holes 1, 2, 4, 7, 9, 10,
  12, 13, 15) the tee ("MPO") is slid along the first leg of the line to match the broadcast number (up to 18 m); this is an
  assumption (a tournament tee back/forward of Gold), not a surveyed pin.
* **Red to Red** (holes 101-118, hole 6 is shared): Red tee to Red basket with UDisc's own bends, par 61 (pars from the old file; the
  UDisc path pars for Red-Red sum to 59, UDisc's layout page says 61, so holes 11 and 12 stay par 4). Path lengths sum to 6,171 ft; UDisc
  lists 5,920 ft for the layout (some Red-Red paths may differ from the layout's), so treat the Red layout lengths as UDisc path distances.

**The videos: what they are.** Both Jomez videos (MPO R1F9 `YkaPk2PR9cM`, 2259 s, holes 1-9; MPO R1B9 `coQSmIg0gxY`, 2534 s, holes
10-18) are player-eye footage with a small course-map graphic: there are NO drone flyovers of the holes. I sampled each hole's tee,
fairway and basket shots frame by frame (canvas contact sheets of paused frames). So trees are what the cameras see beside the
line, not a full inventory, and the second video is shot in fog and rain (holes 14-16 are mostly invisible in it).

**Trees, bushes, ground: procedural, steered by the footage (not surveyed).** Per hole the spec gives belt densities left/right of the line,
open or pinched stretches, the fraction of big spreading live oaks (canopy 3.6-5.2 m, 14-19 m tall) against slash/longleaf pines (canopy 2-2.7 m,
20-26 m), the few trees I could place by eye (large oaks near basket or line), wiregrass clumps (bushes) and the ground features.
Two oaks close the inside corner of every bent line so the direct line is blocked (needed by the lane check). No ponds, bunkers or OB
are used: the sandy pit floor on hole 6 and the marsh grass seen on the hole 8 intro are NOT modelled as water.

**Elevation: estimates.** There is no survey, lidar or flyover. Heights are my reading of the footage (tee boxes above or below the
fairway, descents, steps, banks), expressed as stations along each line and blended into the height grid (5 m cells, soft fall-off
about 35-50 m either side, 0.2 m of gentle undulation), then chained so each hole starts near the previous basket. Total relief about 5.8-22 m.
Treat stated drops as plus or minus 50 %:

| Hole | Tee to basket (est.) | Features read from video |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | -2 m | built-up turf pad, sandy lane, big oaks at the basket (medium) |
| 2 | -7.5 m | tee at a fence, broad mown lawn dropping into a pine chute, basket low (medium) |
| 3 | -1 m | trail dipping into a shallow hollow, dense woods (medium) |
| 4 | -1.5 m | tee on an embankment (+1.5), fence and open ground left (medium) |
| 5 | +2 m | clearing then a sandy knoll under the basket (+2.5) (medium) |
| 6 | -5 m (tee box +5 over the pit floor, basket +0.8) | wooden elevated tee, sand pit floor (medium) |
| 7 | -2.5 m | open sandy tee, big oak at right (medium) |
| 8 | -1 m | basket under a bank with wooden stairs left (+4.5) (medium) |
| 9 | -5 m | turf tee over a wooded gully, banks +2.5 either side (medium) |
| 10-12 | about 0 | trail / clearing (low-medium) |
| 13 | -3 m | quarry bowl, limestone wall about +7.5 m behind the basket (low-medium) |
| 14-18 | about 0 to -1 | 17 has a low ledge and stone steps at the basket; 18 has a raised wooden tee (+2) (low to low-medium) |

**Per-hole confidence.** Geometry (tee, basket, bends, length, par) is high on every hole. Trees/ground: 1-9 medium (seen on video,
positions of individual trees are not), 10-13, 15, 17, 18 low-medium, 14 and 16 low (UDisc line only; fog / not studied).
Not done: tracing canopies from UDisc satellite screenshots as for Picnic Island (the video-steered procedural trees replace it), a flyover
check (none exists in these videos), and any pixel-level comparison of the top-down previews with the video stills (only a rough look at the previews against the
frames). Checks run: lane check (LaneCheck.kt) passes for all 36 holes, loader/length/par checks of BundledCoursesTest pass (file 137 KB).

## Which Cypress is on the Pro Tour?

None found. Cypress Point Park hosted the Gulf Coast Tour Challenge (PDGA Pro/Am C-Tier, 2022-2024), which is not DGPT:
https://www.pdga.com/tour/event/65993 , https://www.pdga.com/tour/event/79430 . The 2026 DGPT schedule lists no Tampa
or Cypress event; its only Florida stop is the Supreme Flight Open at Olympus, Brooksville. The "Tampa Cypress" the user
may mean is probably Cypress Point Park (the only Tampa Cypress disc golf course found); the Pro Tour Florida course is Olympus.

## Hole shaping (doglegs, blockers, hazards)

Every bundled course except Picnic Island and Olympus was reshaped so that holes are not all straight tee-to-basket shots: doglegs
(via points with trees filling the inside corner), S-curves, tall blocking trees, bush walls, a few ponds and out-of-bounds
strips, tight tree tunnels. This is INVENTED shaping done by tools/shape_holes.py. It is not taken from the real
courses: for Cypress Point Park only the lengths, pars and approximate tee/basket positions stay as described
above (tee-to-basket distance and par are unchanged; the playing line is a few percent longer on doglegs), and the water,
OB and bends there should not be read as real features. (Olympus was rebuilt separately, see its section.) The four "inspired" courses were already invented.
